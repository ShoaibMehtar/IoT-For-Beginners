01 — About Q Ventus

Engineering Excellence for Critical Environments

Q Ventus is an HVAC engineering company specialising in systems where reliability is not optional — commercial buildings, industrial facilities, offshore platforms and marine vessels.

Our engineers and certified technicians design, install, commission and maintain cooling, ventilation and refrigeration systems to exacting technical and safety standards. We build long-term partnerships with our clients, supporting their plant through its entire lifecycle with preventive maintenance and rapid emergency response.
