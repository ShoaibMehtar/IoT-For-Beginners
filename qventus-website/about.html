Your Trusted HVAC Engineering Partner

Q Ventus is a professional HVAC engineering company trusted to design, maintain and optimise mission-critical cooling systems for commercial, industrial, marine and offshore facilities.

Who we are

Not simply an air conditioning contractor

We exist for the facilities where HVAC reliability is critical: hotels that cannot lose guest comfort, hospitals with clinical requirements, cold stores that cannot lose stock, and offshore modules that must stay pressurized.

Our services span onshore and offshore sectors — HVAC design and engineering, installation, commissioning, preventive maintenance and emergency support — tailored to the specific needs of each client. In the offshore sector our technicians are certified and trained to Qatar Petroleum standards, with strong expertise in cabin air conditioning engineering and installation.

Behind every project is a diverse team of engineers, technicians, supervisors and managers. We invest in them through fair wages, training and safety systems — because skilled, safe people are what make engineering reliable.

How we work

Engineering-led, from survey to long-term support

One accountable team across the full lifecycle of your system.

  1. AssessSite survey, condition assessment and understanding of your operational risk.
